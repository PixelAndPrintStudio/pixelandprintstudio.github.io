What counts as different

A morning that starts hours late. A kitchen that stays quiet through a mealtime. Movement at 3 AM that does not settle. A camera that stops reporting. Ordinary days are never narrated. Only the change is.

The promise

Porchlight does not watch your parent's house. It tells you how your parent's day went. A flag is never an alarm and never a diagnosis. It is a reason to call, and the call is the point.
