Privacy Policy

Privacy, kept simple.

This page explains, in plain language, what this website does and does not do with your information.

Effective October 18, 2026.

Who we are

Pixel & Print Studio is a small design studio in Virginia. This website is our storefront window: a catalog of the wallpapers, printable art, and digital toolkits we make. It is a static showcase site. There are no accounts here, no sign up forms, no comment sections, and no checkout of our own.

Buying happens elsewhere

When you buy something, you check out on the shop that hosts the product: Ko-fi, Gumroad, Etsy, or, for daily wallpapers, Zedge. Your payment and order details are handled by that shop and governed by its own privacy policy. We never see your card number, and this website collects nothing about a purchase.

What this site collects

Almost nothing, because there is nothing here to collect it with. We run no forms, logins, or trackers of our own on these pages.

Like nearly every website, this one reaches you through a hosting service, and the host keeps standard server logs (the pages requested, browser type, and similar technical details) so the service runs reliably and securely. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so those logs are covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

Advertising and cookies

This site shows ads through Google AdSense. To serve those ads, Google and other third party vendors use cookies, small files stored in your browser, to show advertising based on your prior visits to this site and to other sites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, lets Google and its partners serve ads based on those visits.

You are in control of this. You can opt out of personalized advertising, or adjust how it works for you, in these places:

  • Google Ads Settings, to opt out of personalized ads from Google.
  • Digital Advertising Alliance choices, to opt out of personalized ads from many participating companies at once.
  • Network Advertising Initiative opt out, for its member networks.

Most browsers also let you block or clear cookies in their settings. If you opt out, you may still see ads here, but they will not be picked for you based on your browsing.

Other cookies and analytics

Apart from the advertising cookies described above, this site does not set cookies of its own, and we do not currently run analytics. If that changes, or if anything else about how this site handles data changes, we will update this page and note the new effective date here.

Get in touch

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the messaging tools on any of our shops: Ko-fi, Gumroad, or Etsy. That is the fastest way to get a human answer.
